Aurivane

Noctis Veil

The quiet depth of night, folded inward.

Noctis Veil emerges in the hours when the world softens and sound recedes.

It is not darkness, but comfort — a fragrance that settles close, inviting stillness rather than spectacle.

Worn at night, it becomes private; worn by day, it carries the calm of shadow beneath light.

Olfactive Architecture

TopSmoked air, barely sweet
HeartResinous calm, velvet and slow
BaseDeep woods held close to skin

A presence felt, never announced.
