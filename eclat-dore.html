Éclat Doré

A moment suspended in warm afternoon light.

Éclat Doré opens like the last light of afternoon settling into a room — warm, deliberate, unhurried.

It recalls polished wood, sun-warmed surfaces, and the quiet confidence of time moving slowly, without urgency or display.

A fragrance shaped not by brightness, but by glow — where presence lingers gently, long after the moment has passed.
