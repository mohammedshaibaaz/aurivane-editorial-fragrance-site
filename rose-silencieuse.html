Rose Silencieuse

Soft warmth, spoken only in proximity.

Rose Silencieuse does not arrive loudly.

It unfolds slowly, petal by petal, intimate and restrained — a rose kept close rather than offered.

The fragrance becomes a quiet exchange between skin and memory.

Olfactive Architecture

TopFresh petals, barely sweet
HeartTea rose, warm and hushed
BaseSoft woods, tender and close

A fragrance kept, not shared.
