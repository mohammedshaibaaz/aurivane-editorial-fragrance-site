The house of Aurivane
Quiet radiance, distilled

Where light becomes a signature.

Conceived as an ode to golden hours, Aurivane Éclat Doré was born from a fascination with the way light clings to skin, glass, and fabric as day turns to evening. Each accord is composed to feel like a soft, luminous aura rather than a loud declaration.

Crafted in small, precise batches, the Aurivane collection blends carefully chosen florals, woods, and musks into fragrances that stay close yet linger in memory. From the first bright top notes to the last trace on a cuff, every transition is designed to feel intentional and seamless.

The flacons echo this philosophy—weighty, minimal, and light-washed—serving as quiet objects of desire on the vanity, reflecting the glow of the scent within.

For those who prefer glow over glare.
Origin

Aurivane is composed between Grasse and Paris, where artisans work with rare materials to build luminous, long-lasting signatures in intentionally limited quantities.

Craft

Each bottle is hand-finished, from brushed metallic accents to delicately frosted glass, mirroring the soft sheen of liquid gold.

Philosophy

Fewer fragrances, richer stories: each Aurivane chapter is built to be collected and cherished, not simply worn and replaced.

Edition

Éclat Doré debuts as the house’s signature—your introduction to a growing constellation of golden-hued scents.
