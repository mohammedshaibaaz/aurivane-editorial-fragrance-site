# AURIVANE PARFUMS
## Editorial Fragrance Brand Website

Composed in light. Worn in silence.

An immersive fragrance experience told through 5 chapters. 
Each scent captures a moment between light and shadow.

Experience it: https://mohammedshaibaaz.github.io/aurivane-editorial-fragrance-site/

---

## THE STORY STRUCTURE

AURIVANE isn't just a fragrance catalog. It's a narrative platform.

Five Fragrances. Five Moments. One Philosophy.

Each fragrance lives in a specific time and place:
- Morning light
- Golden hour
- Evening intimacy
- Quiet moments
- Ethereal transitions

The website guides you through these moments like reading chapters in a book.

---

## THE FIVE CHAPTERS

I. ECLAT DORE
A luminous signature. Sun-warmed citrus opening into molten florals, settling 
in a velvet amber base. Like late afternoon light caught on glass.

Top: Bergamot, citrus
Heart: Florals, petals
Base: Amber, creamy woods
For: Golden hour devotees

II. NOCTIS VEIL
Night captured in a bottle. Violet and incense over smoked woods and dark vanilla. 
An intimate embrace, made for whispered conversations.

Top: Violet, incense
Heart: Smoked woods
Base: Dark vanilla
For: After-dark moments

III. LUEUR BLANCHE
Milky light. Creamy florals and soft musk with a breath of cool air. 
Like first light through sheer curtains.

Top: Cool air, linen
Heart: Creamy florals, musk
Base: Pale petals
For: Morning rituals

IV. ROSE SILENCIEUSE
A rose that whispers. Tea-steeped petals, brushed suede, trace of smoke. 
Clings to you like a secret.

Top: Tea rose
Heart: Suede
Base: Smoke
For: Quiet confidence

V. CIEL D'OPALINE
Floating between earth and sky. Airy iris, pearled musk, soft minerals. 
Iridescent. Impossible to pin down.

Top: Airy iris
Heart: Pearled musk
Base: Mineral warmth
For: The undefinable
