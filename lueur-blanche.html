Aurivane

Lueur Blanche

Morning light, untouched and quiet.

Lueur Blanche exists in the earliest hours, before intention takes form.

It carries the softness of light passing through sheer curtains, the air still cool, the world unclaimed.

A fragrance of breath and pause — clean not by sharpness, but by calm.

Olfactive Architecture

TopCool brightness, translucent
HeartMilky florals, weightless
BaseClean musks fading softly

A breath held between night and day.
